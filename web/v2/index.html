    Auto screen-off The screen turns off after a while without a touch or a conversation; a touch, your presence or «Hey Computer» wakes it. Off keeps the screen on all the time; sleep hours still dim it.

    Sleep hours During them, finished background tasks only show a note on screen, they are not read out until you tap the note or wake the device; the screen dims. Waking and conversations work as usual, volume unchanged.
